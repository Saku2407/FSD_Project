Target Audience

This lab is intended for:

  • Undergraduate students of Computer Science, Information Technology and related branches who are learning web development.
  • Students who have just completed the first parts of the Django tutorial and want to understand what happens behind makemigrations and migrate.
  • Self-learners and developers moving from hand-written SQL to an ORM.
  • Teachers who need a structured set of activities on data modelling for a web frameworks course.

Prerequisites: basic Python (classes and functions), a first look at Django (projects, apps and running the development server), and a basic idea of what a relational database table is.
